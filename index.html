Drawing on seawater itself to hold a working elevation, offering another route to combining wave attenuation with tidal power.

02 / PHYSICS

动态载荷抵消

Dynamic Load Cancellation

面向重载动态载荷，在设定工况包络内抑制对悬浮轨道的形变影响；最终判据归独立复核与实测。

For heavy dynamic loads, restraining deformation within a defined envelope; the final word belongs to independent verification and field measurement.

03 / INSTITUTION

开源贡献的数字存证

Digital Provenance of Open Contributions

技术资产以哈希存证，让每一份开源贡献都有独立、可追溯的数字档案；相关国家数据知识产权登记已依法启动，尚在审核。

Technical assets registered as hashes, so each contribution keeps its own traceable record. The related national data-IP registration is lawfully filed and still under review.
