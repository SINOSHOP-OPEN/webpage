Engineering framing (this page follows specification V6.3-r1): wave-attenuation performance is expressed as a transmission-coefficient range derived from scale-model tests and CFD simulation, not as a single fixed percentage; 15 m → 0.6 m is the wave-height ratio of the four-tier system (25×). Fabrication and assembly tolerances follow classification-society building codes (e.g. ABS), with critical connections specified separately against fatigue requirements. In the offshore condition the port uses a mooring positioning system designed to DNV-ST-N001; the standoff distance is governed by the mooring analysis and local water depth. Typhoon submergence, fire protection and lifeboat release are all automatically triggered by hard-wired / local logic control; the blockchain is used only for after-the-fact record-keeping and auditing, and does not participate in the real-time decision loop of safety actions.
