🌊 Constant-Height Tidal Generation Module Dataset · Reproducible
Modularized Constant Tidal Power Generation Technology — zero-energy generation from a naturally rising/falling water surface, while the floating body stays at constant height. Every conclusion is script-verified; datasets and engineering drawings are downloadable for cross-check.

① Zero energy input

The tank is a free surface that finds its own level by water head — no pump, no external force, no controller. Make-up and draw-down are driven by the physical imbalance itself. That is where "zero energy input" comes from.

② Generation by natural flow

The make-up / draw-down flow is the water stream that drives the turbine in the duct — natural flow, never forced. No absolute generation figures are published on this page or in the film.

③ Constant height

Draw-down / make-up cancels the buoyancy imbalance caused by changing submerged wall section. Under a 3.784 m tidal range the body displacement stays < 0.09 m — better than the specified target < 0.15 m.

Geometry & three-value interlock

840 m³ · net volume of the tidal generation tanks Specified value. MOD-STD: 10 columns / 5 walls = 5 groups = 20 tanks (columns pair one-to-one with walls). MOD-DM: 6 walls / 3 columns = 9 groups.
20 tanks · modularized grouping Every 2 columns + 1 wall = 1 group, 4 tanks per group; 10 columns / 5 walls = 5 groups = 20 tanks. Per-tank volume 42 m³.
42 m³ · per-tank volume (three-value interlock) 840 ÷ 20 = 42.0000 — divides exactly. Change the tank count and 42 changes; change 840 and 42 changes. Only locking 840 and 20 together makes 42 unique.
