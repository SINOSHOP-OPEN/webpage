🚀 Project Overview V3.0

SINOSHOP uses one standard module, a four-layer composite structure, and six application scenarios to realize the progressive design philosophy of “Sea generates power, power sustains ships, ships carry bridges, bridges build cities.” At its core lies the principle of “reality through illusion, technology as the vehicle, symbiosis of man and sea.” All subsystems are strongly coupled via a FreeCAD parametric digital twin framework — no isolated component design.

6 Application Scenarios Cost Reduction 30% ~ 85%
A paradigm-level reconstruction based on the standard floating module, covering the full value loop from nearshore infrastructure to space recovery.
Scenario 1

Floating Berth Foundation

Self-floating decouples cost from water depth. Investment per berth: ¥405–540M (↓30~35%), payback 3–5 years.

Foundation cost ↓100%, seabed survey ↓80%

Scenario 2

Floating Breakwater

Material cost ≤15% of traditional rubble-mound breakwaters, wave dissipation 55%~75%, full-depth adaptive.

Mangrove symbiosis for ecological gain

Scenario 3

Floating Tunnel & Bridge

Cost per km ¥280–350M (only 20% of suspension bridge), deployment speed 3–5× faster.

Life-cycle cost reduced by 70%+

Scenario 4

Marine Energy Hull Buoyancy

Unit cost ≤$500/ton (↓72%), steel usage ↓60%, design life ≥50 years.

SBM ensures unsinkable performance

Scenario 5

Computing Eco-Bridge

Tidal power + seawater direct cooling + modular computing, PUE ≤1.05, green energy self-sufficiency 80%~90%.

Cooling power share ≤3%, zero freshwater consumption

Scenario 6

Marine Recovery & Emergency Support

Honeycomb rubber progressive buffering + unsinkable buoyancy, deployment time from 72h to 5 hours, life-cycle cost reduced to 30% of traditional.

Optional aerospace-grade thermal protection

Integrated payback period shortened from 10–30 years to 3–5 years

Four-layer paradigm reconstruction from materials to philosophy

🔬 Materials

Structural (steel + basalt/bamboo fiber) · Encapsulation (fiber-concrete + alumina ceramic) · Hollow cavity (self-floating) · Buoyancy (honeycomb rubber + SBM) → Lightweight + high-strength + unsinkable

📐 Structure

“Hollow truss with stepped groove structure” ensures every gram of material bears stress — equivalent density 450–650 kg/m³, steel usage reduced by 20%~60%.
