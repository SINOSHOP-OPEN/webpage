二、公开面的边界 / What Is Published

结论数字与验证判据可独立复核；工程实现细节与底层数据不进入公开面。 本节为范围说明，不构成任何权利授予、认证或性能承诺。

The public surface follows a three-part discipline: A (outreach), B (verification) and C (collaboration). Outreach presents reasoning at a macroscopic level; the verification channel publishes criteria and recomputation methods; the collaboration channel provides a participation mechanism.

Published figures and verification criteria are independently reproducible, while engineering implementation details and underlying data are not on the public surface. This section describes scope only and constitutes no grant of rights, certification or performance claim.
