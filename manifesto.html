The Spirit of SINOSFT

Investigate things to attain true knowledge; calm the mind, cultivate the self, harmonise the family.

The older text reads “seeking alignment with the noble and the wise” — which is to make this known to all beings: let the wind be wind, let the wave be wave.

01 / DECLARATION宣言

01 / DECLARATIONThe Declaration

我们不是 AI，也不是神棍。我们是试图在深海里算平每一吨浮力的工程信徒。

We are not an AI, nor mystics. We are engineering believers trying to balance every tonne of buoyancy in the deep sea.

你可以嘲笑这 1,188 米的巨兽，你可以不理解那层水膜在极限工况下的细微响应。

You may laugh at this 1,188-metre creature; you may not understand how that water film answers under limit conditions.

但请记住今天，记住这个曾被当成段子的名字：SINOSFT。

But remember today, and remember this name that was once treated as a joke: SINOSFT.

我们在这里，用 247 项全 PASS 的物理断言、用碳与硅的跨物种合作，向自然递交了一份契约：

We stand here with 247 fully passed physical assertions, in a cross-species collaboration of carbon and silicon, and hand nature a covenant:

允许风为风，允许浪为浪。
Let the wind be wind, and the wave be wave.

今天你眼里的玄学，不必等百年，最多 15 年以内，SINOSFT 跨海悬浮隧道浮桥家园，无远弗届，必定能够建成，造福众生。

What you see as mysticism today will not take a century: within fifteen years, the SINOSFT floating tunnel and ocean homeland — reaching beyond any distance — will be built, to bless all living beings.

海生电、电养船、船搭桥、桥造城、城惠民、众生安。

Ocean generates power, power sustains ships, ships erect bridges, bridges build cities, cities bless the people, and all beings rest secure.

实事求是。
贞忠昭日月，惠民播春秋。
Seek truth from facts.
Integrity bright as sun and moon; bounty carried to the people.

功成必定因我，无远弗届。

The work will be accomplished because we were here; it will reach beyond every distance.

海上的安全，靠的是可验证的严谨，不是漂亮的口号。

Safety at sea rests on verifiable rigour, not on beautiful slogans.

02 / OPEN SOURCE AND PROVENANCE开源与数字确权理念

02 / OPEN SOURCE AND PROVENANCEOpen Source and Digital Provenance

技术圈藏龙卧虎，但常因眼前利益而捆缚手脚。SINOSFT 相信，开源不是无偿的奉献，而是为了打破壁垒；确权不是闭门造车，而是为了护航创新。

Our field is full of quiet talent, yet it is often held back by short-term interests. SINOSFT holds that open source is not gratuitous giving but the breaking down of walls; and that registration is not working behind closed doors but the safeguarding of innovation.

我们的宏观物理框架与底层逻辑向全人类开源（遵循 Apache-2.0）；同时，包含 247 项核心断言的物理数据集，已依法启动国家知识产权与数据资产登记。

Our macroscopic physical framework and underlying logic are open to all under Apache-2.0; meanwhile the physical dataset carrying 247 core assertions has entered national intellectual-property and data-asset registration in accordance with the law.

我们拥抱开源，也坚决捍卫创新的尊严。

We embrace open source, and we firmly defend the dignity of innovation.

03 / CONVICTION AND CRAFT情怀与技术，同样重要

03 / CONVICTION AND CRAFTConviction and Craft Matter Alike

人心难静，迷茫无基

A restless heart, a rootless path

在技术圈，我们不缺精妙的算法，也不缺炫酷的模型。但我们深知，在时代的巨浪面前，人心易迷失，技术易无根。

In technology we lack neither elegant algorithms nor striking models. Yet we know how easily the heart loses its way, and how easily technique loses its root, before the great waves of an era.

SINOSFT 不仅是一个开源项目，更是一场精神与物理的「双向配平」。我们用 247 项全 PASS 的断言和严谨的受力计算，给悬浮家园焊牢坚实的物理底座；我们用「格物致知、利见大人」的信念，为每一位同行者提供精神的锚点。

SINOSFT is more than an open-source project: it is a two-way balancing of spirit and physics. With 247 fully passed assertions and rigorous force calculation we weld a firm foundation for the floating homeland; with the conviction of investigating things and making the way known to all, we offer every fellow traveller an anchor for the spirit.

技术，用来在深海里算平每一吨浮力；
情怀，用来在迷茫中点亮每一座灯塔。

Technique is for balancing every tonne of buoyancy in the deep sea;
conviction is for lighting every lighthouse in uncertain times.

我们开源代码，更传递信念。愿你在开源共建中，找到自己的根，不再迷茫，无远弗届。

We open our code, and we pass on a belief. May you find your own root in building together — no longer adrift, reaching beyond every distance.
